I am a PhD student at HKU, advised by Shenghua Gao and working closely with Yi Ma. From 2020 to 2024, I was a PhD student at ShanghaiTech University. From Winter 2022 to Spring 2023, I interned at MSRA, working with Xin Tong and Jiaolong Yang. I did my undergraduate at SCUT in 2020. I work on 3D computer vision, building AI systems capable of interacting with real-world 3D environments using visual inputs, much like how humans do.
